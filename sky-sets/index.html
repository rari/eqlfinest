How Plane of Sky Sets works

Too much treasure, not enough bag space? Tidy things up with our loot filter.

How to use

  1. Move any Sky quest pieces you need out of Inventory > Storage first (for example Equipment). Items left in Storage are not included in the inventory export.
  2. At the bank in game, open Dragon’s Hoard and run /outputfile inventory after it loads.
  3. Find <Character>_<server>_Inventory.txt in your EverQuest Legends game folder and drop it above — or choose Enter pieces to plan from counts only.
  4. Open Inventory > Storage > Currency and enter your Wind Rune counts.
  5. Use the By boss tab to enter or adjust piece counts (Storage leftovers, extras, or planning without a file).
  6. Follow the Turn In list, or browse Overview for ready, missing, and completed sets.
  7. Share copies a link for your rune totals, owned Sky items, and class priorities (clipboard only — your page stays on the open inventory).
