How Spell Bars works

Manage your EverQuest Legends spell bars offline: edit gem slots, then use the next-upgrade line under each filled gem to see what to scribe next.

Ready for your end-game turn-ins? Plan them with Plane of Sky Sets.

How to use

  1. Open a <Character>_<server>_LO#.ini from your EverQuest Legends folder.
  2. Choose up to three classes so the spell picker stays relevant.
  3. Rename bars, add or remove spell sets, and tap a gem to swap its spell.
  4. Use the “Next upgrade” line under each gem to see what to scribe next.
  5. Share copies a link for the current bar (clipboard only — your page stays on the open loadout).
  6. Download the zip, close the game if it is open, replace the file, then reload in-game.
