How the Travel Guide works

Getting across Norrath in EverQuest Legends often means chaining zone connections. This guide finds a short hop-count route from one zone to another.

Decided where you're going next? Mark the way with our map viewer.

How to use

  1. Choose Classic, Kunark, and/or Velious so locked zones stay out of the path.
  2. Pick a from-zone and to-zone, then hit “Find routes”.
  3. Compare the By Foot, Druid Ports, and Wizard Ports result tabs for the route that works best for you.
  4. Share copies a link for this route (clipboard only — your page stays on the open search).

Routes are hop-count only, provided for quick traveling reference. Succor points and zone size are not considered. All players have free access to Origin and Gather Party AAs in EverQuest Legends.
